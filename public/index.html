Our Story

One small lane, one big adda.

Aapni Chai began with a simple idea — a neighbourhood spot with good chai and the comfort to just sit a while. No fancy show, no long waits — just strong chai and a homely feel.

Every cup is brewed fresh, every snack served hot. People come here for a work break, to meet friends, or just to enjoy chai alone.

— Aapni Chai, where every cup has a story.
